Rolodex — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 11, 2026

K3 Innovate ("we," "us," "our") built Rolodex (the "App") as a local-first, offline app. This Privacy Policy explains what data the App handles and why.

The short version

Rolodex does not have a backend server, does not require an account, and does not send Your Content anywhere. Everything you enter — contacts, recipes, how-to entries, notes, and photos — is stored in a local database on your device only. We cannot see it, and we don't have a way to access it remotely.

What data the App accesses, and why

OCR ("Scan from Photo") runs entirely on-device using Google ML Kit's on-device text recognition model. The photo is not uploaded to Google or anyone else for this feature.

Backup export / vCard export creates a file that you explicitly choose to share (via the OS share sheet, e.g. AirDrop, Files, email) — the App does not transmit this file on its own.

In-app purchases (Rolodex Pro)

Rolodex Pro is a one-time unlock processed through RevenueCat, a third-party subscription/purchase management service, which in turn relies on Apple's StoreKit or Google Play Billing. When you make or restore a purchase, RevenueCat and the platform store may process an anonymous device/purchase identifier to verify your entitlement. We do not receive your name, email, or payment details — those are handled entirely by Apple/Google.

See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy for details on their data handling.

Data we do not collect

We do not use analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs. We do not collect your name, email, location, or device identifiers for tracking purposes. There are no user accounts and no sign-in.

Children's privacy

Rolodex is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since the App has no backend, no data is collected from any user regardless of age.

Data retention and deletion

All data lives in local storage on your device. To delete it:

  • Delete individual cards/rolodexes in-app (moved to Trash, then permanently deleted from there), or
  • Uninstall the App, which permanently removes the local database and any attached photos.

We have no copy of your data to delete on our end, because we never received it.

Your choices

  • Camera/Photos permission — you can decline or later revoke this in your device's Settings; OCR scanning and photo attachment simply won't be available.
  • Notifications permission — you can decline or revoke this; follow-up reminders won't be scheduled, but the rest of the App works normally.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the App changes (e.g. if we add a backend feature in the future, this document will be updated first). Continued use of the App after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
